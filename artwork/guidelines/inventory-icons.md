# Inventory icons

Use the native block-item render for cubic machinery. The vanilla furnace, dispenser and IC2 blocks show their cap, working panel and side in a small isometric silhouette. Keep the face detail in the center so it survives the reduction to the inventory grid. Check Survival, Creative, hotbar, held items, drops and NEI with the same item ID.

Use transparent 16×16 sprites for portable gear. Minecraft items often use a diagonal or a small asymmetric detail to establish their silhouette. For the mask keep the glass and leather visible as distinct shapes. For the oxygen tank emphasize the metal cylinder, strap and valve. Keep the flipper pair legible beside IC2 Rubber Boots with a tapered blade and darker boot cuff.

Inspect the native size first. Use 8× nearest-neighbor for edge pixels and ensure the occupied pixels have hard alpha. Preview the worn layers separately. The mask uses the helmet UV, the cylinder occupies the back of the chest UV and the flippers use the boot UV.
