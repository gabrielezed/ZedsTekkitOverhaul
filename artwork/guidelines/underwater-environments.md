# Underwater environments

The Abyssal Trench is a cold ocean environment in the visual language of Minecraft 1.2.5. Use 16×16, hard-edged pixels and simple geometry. Establish a slate-blue rock family, fine grey-green sediment and isolated fossil inclusions. Leave rock and sediment quiet enough to cover large areas. Use irregular joint damage and muted mineral deposits across clean and eroded masonry.

Read marine life at a distance through shape. Kelp forms narrow continuous stalks with uneven olive blades, ribbon algae forms low curling tufts, and fan coral spreads muted rose branches from a narrow base. Tube sponges use desaturated ivory rims and visible openings. Ghost anemones place the brightest cyan-grey detail at the base of a short radial silhouette. Keep transparency binary and empty regions clean.

Limit bright faces and preserve dark water between landmarks. Use localized, weak native block light. Reserve stronger enderic imagery for later ruins and encounters. Review tile seams, projected cubes, silhouettes and sparse arrangements before testing deep and shallow water in the pinned client. Keep original environmental pixels in the shared artwork profiles.
