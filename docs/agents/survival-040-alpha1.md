# Survival 0.4.0-alpha.1 — Site-directed perimeter approach

Opt-in singleplayer candidate for Tekkit Classic 3.1.2. The [authoritative specification](survival-040-spec.md) defines the approved first-alpha boundary and preserves later proposals. The recognized home becomes a stable objective. Walls, openings and terrain shape the encounter, and a sealed perimeter can defeat it without structural loss.

## Admission and target

`LocalLunarCalendar` ticks the existing settlement service before the encounter consumer. `SiegeDirector` owns the single major admission decision at the natural Overworld full-moon dusk. A pending plan reserves that opportunity in memory. Before the first legacy ground-wave tick, successful geometry, exposure, crowding, ground, collision and block-light checks can commit the boundary to the site. A failed preflight falls back to the ordinary assault, whose first ground wave still starts at dusk +100. An ordinary assault with a deferred decision can deliver its initial cue and reserve its aerial slots slightly after dusk. A pause holds an existing plan at the same simulation time. Resynchronization cancels the pending decision. An interrupted committed siege keeps its boundary.

Use `experimentalSiteSieges=true` with `playerAssault=true` to opt in. Otherwise the branch preserves the default player assault path. The seed selects one residue of three logical cycles. Each cycle spans eight Minecraft days, so there is one seed-eligible dusk every 24 days under a continuous unadjusted calendar. At 20 ticks per second this is eight real hours of unpaused simulation. A newly recognized site can be in range for the upcoming opportunity or wait almost 24 days, averaging about 12 days only if recognition times are uniformly distributed. Presence, coverage, geometry, crowding, and difficulty reduce successful siege frequency. The 16-day raw-time cooldown is shorter than the normal 24-day opportunity interval. It still guards the site if the calendar is administratively realigned. The seed and logical cycle produce a reproducible result. Save/reopen does not reroll the dusk, and the calendar and major boundary watermarks reject replay. Fixture alignment supplies genuine natural dusk events in disposable worlds without a production force command.

A committed site waits up to 900 ticks from dusk for native twilight to pass. By dusk +1000 it must enter the approach or resolve. It reruns placement checks before insertion, allows up to twenty first-placement vanilla light rolls at four-tick spacing, and can end without an inserted mob if conditions deteriorate. The committed boundary and cooldown remain in effect.

`SiteSiege` discovers recognized sites through `SiteQueries.nearby` within 24 blocks. It ranks usable parts by distance to their bounds with the save-local site ID as tie-break. It evaluates at most two parts. A dormant, stale or unavailable site cannot admit. A PARTIAL site can supply a usable part when the approach itself verifies in live loaded terrain. The admission observation carries site, dimension and session. The controller resolves those identities and the selected part again before insertion and during the encounter. Player position supplies initial discovery, Survival eligibility and a 48-block simulation range from the selected part.

## Geometry and movement

`SiteApproach` evaluates up to twelve straight cardinal lanes per part. The start is fourteen blocks from the part face and outside the horizontal envelope plus a two-block exclusion margin. A second nearby query checks other recognized envelopes at insertion. A route can reach an open doorway or end at verified exterior frontage. It uses the actual loaded heightmap, standing-height clearance, solid ground and open sky at the insertion column. The native door state and collision shape affect route clearance. Cold, covered, unloaded or uncertain plans defer or fail closed. Protected Interior must report EXPOSED before every insertion. EXPOSED alone gives no permission to spawn.

The final placement reuses native ground, crowding, collision, liquid and ordinary hostile spawn checks. It allows at most three stock skeletons, one group and seven insertion attempts at twenty-tick spacing. The local hostile cap is 28. The site does not claim aerial slots or tactical Enderkin. Existing player assaults keep their configured placement and composition. Site admission allocates no lunar aerial budget. Patrols and ordinary ecology retain their separate schedulers.

`PerimeterApproachTask` attaches to stock skeletons through the verified MCP and pinned client action-task field. Priority 0 and mutex bits 1, 2 and 4 reserve the action scheduler's movement and attack slots while target selection stays native. The task drives the native move helper toward sequential world-space waypoints. The pinned scheduler selects tasks, calls start callbacks, then dispatches updates. The release guard handles a route cleared between selection and update. The task never sets position after initial insertion. A route can enter an open threshold after starting outside. Solid collisions, support and observed obstruction bound progress. The controller releases the approach task on arrival, allowing normal skeleton combat and despawn. Encounter termination releases all remaining tasks and discards tracking. Ordinary loaded or saved skeletons retain their native lifecycle.

The straight, level lane model supports suitable surface entrances and frontage. Stairs, irregular turns, unsupported legacy block shapes, and vertical or underground approaches commonly defer or fall back. Closed walls can yield a frontage objective on their exterior. A blocked lane never authorizes an interior insertion.

## Persistence and budgets

The existing `data/zto_player_assault.dat` uses its original `Boundary` and `Open` tags. A versioned optional `SiteSiege` compound stores the last site ID, original admission boundary, last outcome, and up to 128 site cooldowns. Each commitment reserves at least 384,000 raw ticks for that site. The consumed boundary rejects replays after rewind or reload. Expired cooldowns are pruned on new commitments, and a full live table defers further site admissions. Legacy data without the optional compound loads normally. A malformed siege extension disables siege admission while keeping the valid major watermark and player assault path.

On reopening an open siege, the candidate records `INTERRUPTED_ON_REOPEN` and retains the cooldown and consumed boundary. It persists no route, snapshot or mob pointer. During play it resolves on attacker loss, exhausted insertion, stopped progress, unexpected route obstruction, six hundred unpaused ticks, player absence, Peaceful, site invalidation, world changes or lunar cancellation. Once all inserted attackers reach frontage, normal local combat can continue and the encounter resolves after at least 240 ticks.

Planning runs on the owning world thread. It samples one lane at a time, up to 24 lanes and 40 preflight samples. A geometry sample has ceilings of 28 standing columns, one heightmap lookup and 255 direct sky-cell reads. A standing check queries support and up to two body cells, with bounded native metadata and shape lookups for doors. Measurements count native world block and metadata calls separately from direct chunk sky reads. Protected Interior uses its existing idle recognition slot and 169-read ceiling. Active route checks run every five ticks with up to three points per live approach task. A reinforcement tick spends that sample on the full lane. Each of up to three skeletons receives at most one siege move command per native AI tick. The planner performs no general pathfinder search or synchronous chunk generation. Native entity updates, including ordinary AI after release, continue under the vanilla lifecycle.

## Diagnostics and verification

Open **Numpad 1 → Site Siege**. The monitor reports the major encounter kind, upcoming seed-eligible cycle and last dusk, planning and native spawn wait states, active site ID and last sampled coverage, site session and dimension, insertion and destination, objective progress, active and inserted attackers, remaining budget, elapsed approach ticks, outcome and cooldown. Active coverage comes from the simulation's world-thread queries. The idle and terminal views label retained geometry as the last site. The monitor reads snapshots and does not request new geometry. The immutable snapshot also exposes peak attackers, movement progress, geometry age, lane and sample counts, protection reads and planner timings. Normal gameplay receives no overlay.

Run `zto-tools/test-zto-agent.ps1 -Focus site-siege` after building. The runner uses marked disposable worlds and the existing natural lunar listener, pinned native entity logic and actual save storage. For deterministic admission it computes an eligible cycle in the fixture and begins at the tick before dusk. The production game exposes no force-siege command. Run `zto-tools/test-zto-agent.ps1 -Pack` for the full headless compatibility and Survival suite. See [test tiers and graphical host restriction](testing.md).

- **Persistence.** Legacy NBT and malformed siege data preserve the major boundary. The optional version-1 extension stays bounded at 128 cooldown rows. Reopen, Peaceful, elimination, detach, native Nether world replacement and lunar resync passed. The dimension test exercises the controller with a native Nether world and returns to the Overworld. ## Owner walkthrough

1. Use a disposable Tekkit Dev copy with the staged review artifact. Set `experimentalSiteSieges=true` and `playerAssault=true` in `minecraft/config/ZedsTekkitOverhaul.cfg` with Minecraft closed. Start in Survival in the Overworld on Normal or Hard. Use flat ground and keep the exterior staging lane dark. A supported lane leads straight to the center of a wall or two blocks to either side. Leave at least 14 level blocks outside the house face and keep yourself at least 12 blocks from the insertion end during preflight.
2. For a new reproducible world, enter the nonzero seed `3`. On a new aligned calendar its first full moon is cycle 0, logical day 4, at tick-of-day 13000. On existing worlds open **Numpad 1 → Lunar Cycle** for the current logical day, cycle, phase and time, then **Site Siege** for the next seed-eligible cycle. Let the chosen dusk occur with time advancing naturally. The Debug Hub cannot force a siege. Copy the disposable save with Minecraft closed before that boundary to run independent variants.
3. Build a roofed house with space to walk, spend time inside, and use a chest, furnace or workbench to corroborate occupation. Open **Numpad 1 → Settlement Recognition → Assessment** and confirm a recognized positive site ID before dusk. At each run note the save seed, cycle, raw dusk, site and part bounds, difficulty and expected entrance. From the Site Siege view record the selected ID, coverage, approach coordinates, insertion and arrival counts, outcome and cooldown. Record actual movement and the state of the entrance in screenshots or a short clip when possible.

## Next integration points

The director can arbitrate later tactical profiles within the same boundary. A later planner can propose alternate objectives through the same immutable site observations and action-specific geometry checks. Breaching, industrial infiltration, aerial participation, new enemies, advanced groups and multiplayer require separate approval and validation. Owner acceptance should cover gameplay pacing, actual wall and entrance behavior, normal play without diagnostic overlays, and monitor readability across GUI scales.
